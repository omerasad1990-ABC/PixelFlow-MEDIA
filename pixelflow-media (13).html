Privacy Policy — PDPA Compliance

PixelFlow Media collects and processes personal data (name, email, brand assets, payment details) solely to deliver design services to our clients.

In line with the Personal Data Protection Act, we:

  • Only collect data necessary to deliver your project
  • Never sell or share client data with third parties
  • Store data securely with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Allow clients to request data access, correction, or deletion at any time

For any data requests, contact our team through the Smart Assistant or your account manager.

Security & Cyber Hygiene

We maintain baseline cyber hygiene practices across our infrastructure to protect client data and design assets:

  • Encrypted payment gateway for all transactions
  • Role-based access control for internal systems
  • Regular software and dependency updates
  • Routine backups of client project data

Security is treated as an ongoing practice, not a one-time setup.

Terms of Service — Copyright
